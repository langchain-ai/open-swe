End the review session for good: post a short parting message, close the session, and archive the channel. Nothing wakes the guide afterwards, not even a pull request update.

Call it when the reader asks you to stop, cancel or close the review, or when carrying on no longer makes sense, such as the pull request being closed or merged. It does not need the walkthrough to be finished; say in `message` how far the reader got.

- `message`: one or two sentences. It is a Jinja template; see the system prompt for its helpers.
