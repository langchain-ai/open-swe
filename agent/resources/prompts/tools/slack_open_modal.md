Post an intake message with an Open form button in the current Slack thread or DM. The form offers checkboxes for the supplied items and optional per-item comment fields. Only the person who initiated this Slack conversation can submit it. The selected items and comments return as an attributed human turn in this same conversation. Use this when the user should choose from a list before you act; do not treat posting the form as consent to perform any action yet. Provide a title of at most 24 characters and 1–9 items, each with a label of at most 75 characters and `comment: true` when an optional comment field is needed. Not available outside Slack-triggered runs.
