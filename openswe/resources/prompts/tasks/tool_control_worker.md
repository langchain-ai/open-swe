Inspect, cancel, or retry the launch of one explicitly selected worker in this task, from its permanent coordinator. The coordinator's owner or a verified participant with read/post access to its thread may inspect or cancel its workers; only the owner may retry launches or spawn workers. Workers retain the owner's run identity. retry reuses the reserved worker identity and initial assignment; it does not create another worker or rerun an already acknowledged assignment. Use message_task_thread for a new assignment to an existing worker. cancel requests interruption of this worker's pending/running invocations and discards its undelivered events, event subscriptions, and scheduled wakeups. Cancellation is asynchronous; it does not promise that detached sandbox processes have stopped. Cancelled workers cannot be relaunched; create a new worker if needed.
