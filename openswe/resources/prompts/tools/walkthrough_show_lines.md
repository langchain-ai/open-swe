Show the reader lines cut just for them, outside the plan, with your title and explanation and a "Next" button. Use it when they ask to see something the plan's chunks do not fit, such as a whole function split across chunks or one change across several files.

- `title`: roughly 4-10 words, identifiers in `backticks`.
- `show`: the lines, per `path`, with `added` and `deleted` ranges numbered as for `walkthrough_plan_chunk`. Any lines the reader has not approved or skipped can go in, planned or not.
- `explanation`: two to four plain sentences. The code is rendered from the lines, so never include it.

Approving it counts those lines as reviewed wherever they sit in the plan. A chunk already on screen and not yet approved goes back to the reader's list. After it posts, end your turn and wait for the reader.
