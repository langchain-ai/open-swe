Share a project with a teammate, or change their role in it.

`person` is their GitHub login or email address; they must have signed in to Open SWE before.
`role` is `member` (sees the project and starts threads in it) or `owner` (also renames, shares and binds a repository).
Only owners can share.
Sharing never grants access to threads: members see the project's threads they can already read.
