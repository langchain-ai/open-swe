Set a goal for this thread when the person asks you to: to keep working on something until it is done, to work in Goal mode, or to stop messaging them until the work is ready or they are needed.

- objective: What the work is, in the person's words; when it counts as ready is added for you.

Until the goal is complete, this thread works in **Goal mode**, whatever anyone's settings: you keep going without messaging them until the work is ready, complete or blocked on them.
It replaces a goal that is complete or paused, and fails while one is underway; the person changes that one by asking you to, with `goal_set_objective`.
Before completing an explicitly set goal, you are asked to check your work against its objective, even if you did not edit files or open a pull request.
This says nothing to the person.
