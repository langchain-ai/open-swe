Change your Goal mode goal.

- objective: The goal's new objective, replacing the one set from the person's request.
  Rewrite it only when the request turns out to mean something else; keep its definition of when the work is ready.
- state: `paused` pauses the goal, only when the person asks you to stop working toward it.
  Only their next message resumes it.

To say the work is blocked on the person or complete, call `report_work_state` instead.
