Manage an Open SWE thread without deleting it. Use resolve/unresolve to close or reopen it, and cancel to stop a run. For a full handoff, use action="send_message" with replace_current=true: after successful dispatch, resolve the current thread and detach its Slack listener, leaving the target as the active conversation. Both threads must be accessible to the requester, and the target must differ from the current thread. History is retained, not merged or deleted. On a cleanup failure, dispatched=true means the target already received the message; do not resend it. After a successful replacement, send only a final pointer to the target and stop working in the old thread.
