Record your decision on one pending engram.

`adopted` means the workspace's prompt now carries what it says, in your own words, or already did; it then stops applying to its person as their own engram.
`declined` means it stays out of the prompt, and a personal one keeps applying to its person.
Decide only after you published any prompt the decision depends on.

Args:
- engram_id: The engram's id.
- decision: `adopted` or `declined`.
- note: One line on why, for whoever later asks why the prompt says what it says.
